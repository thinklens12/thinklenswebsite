Our Story

Built on
precision.

Founded in 2025, Thinklens Consulting LLP was established to bridge the gap between complex data needs and seamless offshore execution. We don't just "staff" projects — we provide a compliant, high-caliber extension to your technical team.

In our very first year, we became the trusted sub-contracting partner for projects at some of the world's largest organizations — spanning Aerospace & Defense, Biomedical Devices, and Consumer Packaged Goods.

We are a fully registered Indian LLP, IEC-authorized for international service trade, making us the ideal silent partner for overseas staffing agencies looking to scale delivery capacity without administrative overhead.

We specialize in the "messy" middle of data — cleanup, migration, and modernization — ensuring Fortune 500-level standards are met on every single engagement.
