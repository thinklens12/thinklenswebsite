How we got here

Built on what
the market was missing.

Thinklens was founded in 2025 after years of watching teams struggle to get senior data and IT expertise fast enough. Agencies absorbed the cost of building a bench; freelancers turned down work they couldn't cover alone; businesses waited out long hiring cycles for help they needed in weeks. Every gap showed up downstream — in cleanup work, missed deadlines, or churn.

We built Thinklens to be the answer to that gap: a senior team you can switch on for a sprint or a year. For staffing agencies we plug into your MSA without competing for the client; for freelancers we're a second pair of senior hands; for businesses we're expertise without the headcount. Our consultants are vetted for Fortune 500-grade rigor in data governance, ETL testing, BI, and SAP/Workday data work — plus the project support, ongoing support, and training that keep it all running.

We don't try to be everything. We make sure the work you need delivered gets staffed, delivered, and signed off — on time, on spec, and at the technical depth your client expects — then we step back.
