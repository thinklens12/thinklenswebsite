How we got here

Built on what
the market was missing.

Thinklens was founded in 2025 after years of watching staffing agencies absorb the cost of building their own data and BI bench. Every new client engagement meant rehiring, retraining, or stretching delivery teams thin — and every gap in technical depth showed up downstream in cleanup work, missed deadlines, or client churn.

We built Thinklens to be the answer to that gap: a senior consulting team that plugs into your existing MSA without competing for the client relationship. Our consultants are vetted for Fortune 500-grade rigor in data governance, ETL testing, BI modernization, and SAP/Workday data work — the exact disciplines staffing partners are most often asked to deliver and least often staffed for.

We don't grow the agency for you. We make sure every engagement you sell can be staffed, delivered, and signed off — on time, on spec, and at the technical depth your end client expects.
