Govern the data.

Governance frameworks that hold up under audit, and Technical Business Analysts who speak both SQL and stakeholder — data stewardship, master data management, and requirements work delivered offshore under your MSA.

Data governance is the set of ownership, definitions, lineage, and quality controls that keep data trustworthy and audit-ready; a Technical Business Analyst is the bridge who turns business questions into validated data specifications. Thinklens delivers both offshore under your MSA. Governance work includes stewardship frameworks (data-domain ownership, RACI, business glossaries), master data management with golden-record and survivorship rules across SAP MM and Workday domains, policy and source-to-report lineage mapped to GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 controls, and quality KPIs published on a stewardship dashboard. Thinklens Technical BAs don't hand requirements over a wall — they profile the actual source tables, draft conceptual and dimensional models with source-to-target mappings, and prototype the target report in SQL, Power BI, Tableau, or Spotfire before a sprint is committed. The result: ambiguity resolved in discovery, not discovered in UAT.

Completeness, validity, and freshness metrics per domain, published on a stewardship dashboard — governance you can see slipping before it becomes a data incident.
