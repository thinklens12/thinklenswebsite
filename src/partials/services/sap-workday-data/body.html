Thinklens provides offshore data services for SAP and Workday — the stewardship, migration testing, master-data cleanup, and downstream BI that decide whether a rollout delivers value. Across SAP S/4HANA, ECC, and MM, the team runs pre-migration profiling, conversion-rule and mock-cutover testing, post-go-live data validation, steady-state stewardship, and material-master deduplication and harmonization, plus master-data governance frameworks documented in client terminology.
