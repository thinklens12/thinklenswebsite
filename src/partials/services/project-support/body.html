Services / Project Support & Overflow

Extra senior hands,
on call.

When a deadline is close and the right specialist isn't on your bench, Thinklens drops in senior consultants to absorb the spike — remote, fast to ramp, and gone when the crunch is over.

Project support and overflow resourcing is on-demand senior capacity that teams bring in to cover delivery spikes, staffing gaps, or specialist work without making a permanent hire. Thinklens provides this remotely and on short notice: experienced consultants who plug into your existing tools, process, and standards, deliver against a defined scope, and hand the work back cleanly. It suits staffing agencies covering a contract they can't immediately fill, freelancers who need a second pair of senior hands, and businesses facing a deadline crunch. Engagements run on Time & Materials for evolving scope or fixed-scope for defined deliverables, with fast ramp-up and clear documentation so the work outlasts our involvement.

How it works

Fast in,
clean out.

Overflow isn't a long-term hire — and it shouldn't feel like one. Fast to start, easy to end, and invisible to your client.

How quickly can you start?

On short notice — often within days for pre-vetted skills. A short scoping call defines the work, tools, and finish line, and we ramp on your existing stack with no re-platforming.

What engagement terms do you use for overflow?

Time & Materials for evolving scope, or Fixed-Scope for defined deliverables. Overflow is meant to be easy to start and easy to end — no long-term commitment required.

Will you work behind our brand?

Yes. For staffing agencies we deliver silently behind your brand and never compete for your client; for businesses and freelancers we slot into your team and process.
