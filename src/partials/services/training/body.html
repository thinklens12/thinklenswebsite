Practical, hands-on training from people who do the work for a living — Power BI, Tableau, Spotfire, SQL, ETL testing, and data governance. Built around your team's real data, not generic slides.

Training and enablement is structured, hands-on upskilling that helps a team use its data and BI tools confidently and correctly. Thinklens delivers it remotely and on short notice, taught by consultants who deliver the same work in production — so sessions use real-world patterns, not textbook examples. Topics span the BI tools (Power BI, Tableau, TIBCO Spotfire), SQL and data fundamentals, ETL testing and report QA, and data governance and the Technical BA role. Sessions can be scoped as a focused workshop, a multi-day bootcamp, or ongoing enablement, and are built around the customer's own data and use cases wherever possible. It suits staffing agencies upskilling a bench, businesses enabling internal teams, and freelancers sharpening a specific skill.
