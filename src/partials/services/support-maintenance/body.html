Services / Support & Maintenance

Keep the lights on.
We'll maintain it.

The dashboard, pipeline, or system is live — now it needs someone to watch it, fix it, and improve it. Thinklens provides remote, ongoing support so you don't have to hire full-time for it.

Ongoing support and maintenance is the work of keeping live systems running, reliable, and improving after a project ends — monitoring, fixing issues, and making enhancements. Thinklens delivers this remotely for data, BI, and IT systems: refresh and pipeline monitoring, bug fixes, small enhancements, user support, and agreed response times (SLAs). It's the layer most projects forget to budget for — the dashboard that breaks when a source changes, the pipeline that fails silently, the report request that piles up. Rather than carry a full-time hire for intermittent work, agencies, freelancers, and businesses keep a Thinklens support arrangement on tap: predictable cost, senior people who know the system, and continuity when something needs attention.

The work that
starts after go-live.

Support is unglamorous and easy to under-resource. We make it predictable — so the things you've built stay trustworthy.

Monitoring

Refresh & pipeline health

We watch data refreshes, ETL jobs, and report availability — and catch the silent failures (a stale extract, a broken source) before your users do.

Fixes

Bug fixes & incident response

When something breaks — a wrong number, a failed load, a permissions issue — we diagnose to the source and fix it, with a record of what changed and why.

Enhancements

Small changes & requests

The steady stream of "can we add…" requests — a new measure, an extra filter, a tweaked layout — handled without standing up a whole project each time.

SLAs

Agreed response times

Clear severity levels and response windows, so urgent issues get urgent attention and the rest is scheduled — no guessing about when something will be looked at.

Questions,
answered.

What do you support?

Mainly the data and BI stack — Power BI, Tableau, and Spotfire dashboards, ETL pipelines, and warehouses (including SAP and Workday feeds) — plus the adjacent IT systems that keep them fed and running.

How does pricing work for support?

Typically a retainer or block of hours with agreed response times, so urgent issues get urgent attention and routine requests are scheduled. We size it to your actual volume.

Do you offer SLAs?

Yes — clear severity levels and response windows agreed up front. We only commit to what we can reliably support, and we'll tell you when something is out of scope.
