Common questions from agencies, freelancers, HR partners, and businesses evaluating Thinklens for short-term, overflow, or long-term work.

Do you work as a sub-contractor under our MSA?

Yes. Thinklens operates as a silent offshore delivery partner under your Master Service Agreement — you keep the client relationship, branding, and invoicing. We sub-contract on T&M, Fixed-Scope, or B2B Freelance Consultant arrangements depending on how your client is billed.

What time-zone overlap do you offer for US, UK, and APAC clients?

Our consultants run flexible shifts covering 3–5 hours of daily overlap with US Eastern, UK, and Singapore working hours. For critical delivery phases, sprint reviews, or production go-lives, we align fully with your client's working day.

Which BI tools do you specialize in?

Enterprise delivery on Power BI, Tableau, and TIBCO Spotfire — dashboard development, report modernization, semantic modeling, performance tuning, and end-to-end Report QA. Most engagements also involve SQL-heavy data preparation and ETL Testing.

Do you handle SAP and Workday data work?

Yes. We support SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC, SAP MM, and Workday Data — covering data stewardship, master data cleanup, migration testing, and downstream BI reporting. Typical engagements pair a Technical BA with a senior data engineer.

What does your ETL Testing and Report QA process cover?

End-to-end ETL pipeline validation: source-to-target reconciliation, transformation logic, referential integrity, regression testing, and data-quality gates. For BI reports, we validate calculations, security models, drill-through paths, and visual accuracy across Power BI, Tableau, and Spotfire — typically reaching 95%+ test coverage before sign-off.

Can you provide a Technical Business Analyst (BA) for our project?

Yes. Our Technical BAs lead requirements gathering, source-system analysis, data-model design, and stakeholder workshops — with hands-on SQL and BI tool fluency so they can prototype and validate without waiting on engineering.

What's your delivery base and which compliance frameworks do you follow?

India-based, authorized under the Indian Importer Exporter Code (IEC) for international trade in services. We align to client-side controls — including GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 — and operate inside your secure VDI/VPN environment when required. Each engagement signs an NDA before kickoff.

How are engagements priced — T&M, Fixed-Scope, or retainer?

All three. We offer Time & Materials for evolving scopes, Fixed-Scope for well-defined deliverables, MSA Sub-Contracting for ongoing partnerships, and B2B Freelance Consultant arrangements for short engagements. Pricing is shared transparently in the first call.

How quickly can a consultant start on a new engagement?

Typically 1–2 weeks from signed SOW. For pre-vetted skill sets — Power BI, Tableau, SQL, ETL Testing, Technical BA — we can often start within 5 business days.
