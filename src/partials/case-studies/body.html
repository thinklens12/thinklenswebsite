Master data that
survives an audit.

A global aerospace organization, reached through a staffing-agency partner, needed its material and supplier master data brought under governance ahead of a compliance review.

Challenge

Ungoverned masters, looming review

Years of decentralized data entry across SAP MM had left duplicate materials, inconsistent units, and supplier records nobody owned — with an audit on the calendar.

What we did

Stewardship + cleanup, offshore

A Technical BA mapped data-domain ownership and definitions; a senior data consultant profiled the masters, ran dedup with agreed survivorship rules, and documented lineage for every change.

Outcome

Audit-ready, owned, monitored

Clean master data with named stewards, a living business glossary, and quality KPIs on a dashboard — the review passed without a data finding against the programme.

Reports built to
clinical standards.

A leading medical-device innovator needed its quality and compliance reporting modernized — without disturbing the validated processes regulators had already signed off.

Challenge

Legacy reports, regulated data

Aging Spotfire and Excel reporting on quality data, where every change had to be traceable and every figure defensible to a regulator — speed could not come at the cost of control.

What we did

Modernize with a QA gate

Rebuilt the reporting layer in Power BI and Tableau with documented source-to-target mappings, and ran every report through our Report QA matrix — calculations, security models, and drill-throughs validated against known totals.

Outcome

Modern stack, defensible numbers

Clinical and compliance teams moved to maintained, validated dashboards — with a test evidence trail that satisfied both internal quality and the agency partner's end client.

One truth across
every region.

A global CPG organization ran multi-region product reporting on ETL pipelines that disagreed with each other — and with the systems of record.

Challenge

Pipelines that disagreed

Regional teams maintained parallel ETL flows with diverging business rules — the same product line reported different volumes depending on which dashboard you opened.

What we did

Reconcile, consolidate, test

Source-to-target reconciliation across regions, a consolidated transformation layer with versioned business rules, and a regression suite so the next change couldn't silently fork the numbers again.

Outcome

A single reporting layer

Regional dashboards consolidated onto one tested pipeline — finance, supply chain, and commercial teams reading from the same numbers, with quality gates at ingest.
